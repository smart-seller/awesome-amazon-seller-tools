## Impendulo Nokubuyekezwa
- [AMZFinder](https://www.amzfinder.com/) - I-AMZFinder iyinkundla ezimele yokubuyekeza nokuqhathanisa amathuluzi abathengisi base-Amazon. Ihlela amathuluzi kuzo zonke izinqubo eziyisisekelo eziyi-8 (ucwaningo lomkhiqizo, ucwaningo lwamagama angukhiye, ukuthuthukiswa kohlu, ukubeka kabusha amanani, i-PPC, ukuhlaziya, nokunye), inikeza ithuluzi ngalinye amaphuzu ngendlela engaguquguquki, futhi incoma izitaki ezakhelwe ngaphambili ngokwesigaba semali engenayo yomthengisi — isiza abathengisi ukugwema imibhalisombhalo ephindaphindiwe nokuthola amathuluzi afanele ebhizinisini labo.
- [FeedbackExpress](https://www.feedbackexpress.com/) - I-FeedbackExpress isiza abathengisi base-Amazon nabaneziteshi eziningi ukukhulisa ukubuyekezwa okuhle nokuphatha impendulo kuzo zonke izimakethe ezifana ne-Amazon, i-eBay, i-Trustpilot, nezinye. Inezici zezicelo zokubuyekeza ezikhethiwe ezihlakaniphile, ukuphathwa okuzenzakalelayo kwempendulo engemihle, kanye nedeshibhodi emaphakathi yokuphatha ukubuyekezwa kuzo zonke iziteshi zokuthengisa.
- [Feedbackwhiz](https://www.feedbackwhiz.com/) - Inkundla yabathengisi base-Amazon nabase-Walmart konke-kunye ehlanganisa izicelo zomyalezo we-imeyili wokubuyekeza/impendulo ezenzakalelayo, izexwayiso zabantu abadukisa uhlu, kanye nokulandelela inzuzo/izindleko. Isekela zonke izimakethe ezinkulu zomhlaba zase-Amazon.
- [Feedback Genius](https://www.sellerlabs.com/products/feedback-genius/?fp_ref=poly61) - I-Feedback Genius eyenziwe yi-Seller Labs yenza ngokuzenzakalela izicelo zokubuyekeza ezivumelana ne-Amazon nokufinyelela impendulo yomthengisi kubathengisi base-Amazon. Ithuluzi lithumela imiyalezo yabathengi ezenzakalelayo lisebenzisa amathempulethi akhiwe ngaphambili noma angokwezifiso, lisindisa abathengisi isikhathi sokulandelela ngesandla.
- [xSellco](https://www.xsellco.com/feedback?utm_source=affiliate&utm_campaign=493ad7f6d045d3dcea1a399c4aa06d27&utm_medium=Link+Share) - I-xSellco iyinkundla yokuhweba kwe-inthanethi exhunyiwe enikeza amathuluzi okusekela amakhasimende (i-eDesk), ukubeka kabusha amanani okuzenzakalelayo, kanye nokuphatha impendulo/ukubuyekezwa kuzo zonke izimakethe ezinkulu ezifana ne-Amazon, i-eBay, i-Walmart, nezinye.

- [River Cleaner](https://rivercleaner.com/) - I-River Cleaner iyisandiso se-Chrome esivumela abathengisi base-Amazon ukuthuthukisa ngokuzenzakalela izinhlu zemikhiqizo. - [Helium 10](https://i.helium10.com/c/1793920/3743859/37271) - Amathuluzi e-Helium 10 aklanyelwe ukonga isikhathi, ukuthola imikhiqizo enenzuzo, ukuthola amagama angukhiye anenzuzo, ukususa amagama angukhiye angadingeki, ukukhomba ukubuyiselwa kwe-FBA kwempahla elahlekile/elimele, kanye nokuthuthukisa izinhlu zemikhiqizo.
